Scan the slip. The record keeps itself.

A player screenshots a slip and the app reads it. Legs are graded from the final score, and the slips build a record by sport and bet type.

Film: a slip screenshot is read; the slip’s true odds show it needs 35.2% to break even, with a margin of 8.9%; the record’s line draws in; an insight card reads ‘You beat the close on 7 of 10 bets.’

You bring four things. We do the rest.

Tells runs beside your platform, not inside it. Bets come in from a screenshot of the slip and are graded from final scores.

You provide

  • Your Apple developer account, so the app is listed under your name
  • Your name, logo, colours and fonts
  • Your legal pages and responsible-gambling message
  • Your odds feed and a live scores feed

We do

  • Build the app in your brand, widgets and Lock Screen included
  • Run its back end, separate from every other brand’s
  • Connect your feeds
  • Test on real devices, submit to Apple and handle App Review
  • Keep it current with iOS, fixes and new features

Safe to put your name on.

What books ask first.

Why a second app?

Your betting app takes the bets; Tells fills the hours around them, under your name. It’s built, so nothing joins your roadmap.

Do the odds tools work against us?

No. They state the margin as a plain figure and never give advice. Players who want these numbers find them elsewhere today; here they get them under your name.

How do we see it working?

In App Store Connect, under your account: installs, sessions and retention, like any app you publish.

Can we run it for more than one brand?

Yes. Each brand gets its own app, theme and back end, from the same code.
